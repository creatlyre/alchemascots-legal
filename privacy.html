Official Compliance Notice: This privacy policy governs data processing, privacy controls, and user account deletion for AlcheMascots: Magic Isle across all supported mobile platforms.

Who we are

AlcheMascots: Magic Isle (the "Game") is provided by CreatLyre / Wojciech Bałdys ("we", "us", or "our"). For privacy questions or requests, contact privacy@alchemascots.com.

Information involved in the Game

  • Game data: Progress, inventory, achievements, settings, purchase-related state, and gameplay or diagnostic events may be stored on your device. If you use cloud synchronization, your game save is also sent to Supabase and associated with your account.
  • Account information: If you sign in with Google, we may receive an account identifier, email address, display name, and profile image URL. Authentication and session information are handled through Google and Supabase.
  • Purchases: Google Play or the applicable app store processes payments. RevenueCat and our services may process purchase identifiers, transaction information, entitlement status, and an app user identifier to verify and restore purchases. We do not receive your full payment card details from the store.
  • Advertising: When ads are enabled or requested, Google AdMob may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and ad interaction or diagnostic information, subject to its settings and applicable consent requirements.
  • Technical information: Our service providers may receive network, device, and operational information when your device connects to their services.
  • Messages to us: If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, including your contact details and request.

How we use information

We use information to operate the Game, save and synchronize progress, authenticate accounts, provide and verify purchases and rewards, serve ads where enabled, troubleshoot problems, protect the service, and respond to requests. Where required by law, we rely on your consent, performance of a contract, our legitimate interests, or compliance with legal obligations, as appropriate to the processing activity. You may withdraw consent where it is the applicable basis.

Service providers

We use or may use the following services when their features are configured and enabled. They process information under their own privacy policies and applicable agreements:

  • Supabase for authentication, cloud saves, and backend services.
  • Google for Google Sign-In, Google Play services and billing, and AdMob advertising.
  • RevenueCat for purchase management and verification.
  • Apple for App Store services and billing on iOS, where applicable.

We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect rights and security. These providers may process information in countries other than your own; applicable safeguards depend on the provider and your location.

Storage and retention

Local game data remains on your device until it is cleared or the app is removed, subject to device backup settings. Cloud account and save data are retained while needed to provide the service, unless deleted earlier or retained for legal, security, or operational reasons. Purchase records, logs, backups, and support messages may be retained for different periods. Our applicable retention periods are: Active accounts are retained until explicit deletion. Upon account deletion request via app or web, cloud save and authentication data are immediately and permanently purged from active databases and fully expunged from system backup cycles within 30 days..

We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices and deletion

You can play with local storage and choose whether to sign in for cloud features, where available. Device and platform settings may offer advertising and privacy controls. Clearing local game data or uninstalling the app may remove progress from your device; it does not necessarily remove cloud data or copies held by service providers.

In the Game's settings, the account-data deletion control clears local storage and attempts to delete the cloud save and sign you out. It does not currently delete the authentication account, and a cloud deletion attempt may fail. To request deletion of your account and associated data, or to ask about access, correction, portability, restriction, or objection where applicable, contact privacy@alchemascots.com. We may need to verify your identity. Some information may be retained where legally required or for legitimate security and operational purposes.

Depending on your location, you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. Google and other providers have their own controls and procedures for data they independently hold.

Children

Parents and guardians with questions about a child's information may contact us at privacy@alchemascots.com. The Game's intended audience, age restrictions, advertising configuration, and any parental consent procedures must be confirmed for the published release and applicable jurisdictions.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game or our practices change. We will update the date above and provide additional notice where required by law.
